SONGSTASH 0.1.0-beta.1

1. What is SongStash?

SongStash is a private, local-first archive for music you have discovered.
Shazam helps identify a song. SongStash keeps a record of your discoveries,
including repeated finds. SongStash is not a replacement for Shazam.

2. Requirements

- macOS
- Apple Silicon Mac

3. Install

1. Unzip the downloaded archive.
2. Move SongStash.app to your Applications folder.
3. Open SongStash.

This beta is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized, so macOS may block the first
launch. If that happens, try opening SongStash once, then open System Settings
> Privacy & Security. Find the message about SongStash, choose Open Anyway,
and confirm that you want to open it.

4. Your data

No SongStash account is required. Your library and settings stay on this Mac
in ~/Library/Application Support/SongStash. SongStash reads your Shazam
history without modifying the Shazam database.

5. What to try

- Import Shazam history or a file, and review the preview before importing.
- Check repeated finds in Find History.
- Add a song manually.
- Search and sort your Library.
- Open songs with your preferred music service.
- Export CSV or JSON and create a backup.
- Try English and Russian, the four themes, and Follow System.

7. Known limitations

- This is beta software for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS.
- The app is not notarized.
- Shazam import depends on Apple's local Shazam database format and may be
  affected by future macOS or Shazam changes.
- SongStash has no cloud sync, built-in music recognition, or automatic updater.

8. If something breaks

Please send feedback through the channel where you received this beta. Include
what you were trying to do, what happened instead, whether it happens again,
and a screenshot if useful. Please also include your macOS version, Mac model
and chip, and approximate number of Shazam history items for Shazam import
issues.

Please do not send your Shazam database, SongStash database, or private music
history unless we agree on that separately.
